Cooking a week of lunches on Sunday costs far less than buying lunch out: one household cut its food spending from about $90 to $35 a week. Pick two base recipes, such as a grain bowl and a soup, cook big batches, and portion them into containers. Roasting vegetables on two sheet pans at once saves time and oven energy. Put the money saved straight into savings so it doesn't drift into other spending.
