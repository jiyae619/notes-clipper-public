title: "How big should an emergency fund be?"

captured: 2026-11-01 09:00
---

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected costs such as a job loss or a car repair. Most planners suggest keeping three to six months of essential expenses in it, in an account you can reach within a day, such as a high-yield savings account. Build it before you start investing: without it, a surprise bill can force you to sell investments at a bad time. If your income is irregular, aim for the upper end, six months.
