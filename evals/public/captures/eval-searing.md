Browning on seared meat comes from the Maillard reaction, which only starts once the surface passes about 140°C (285°F). Water on the surface keeps it near 100°C until it evaporates, so wet meat steams instead of browning. Pat the meat dry with paper towels, heat the pan until the oil shimmers, and don't crowd the pan, which drops its temperature.
